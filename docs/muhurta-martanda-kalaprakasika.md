## 4. The differences and gaps, most important first

**D1. Jupiter and Venus: what is done, what the two texts add or contradict.** The `jupiterVenus` dosha takes the Muhūrta Cintāmaṇi's rule (asta; Capricorn; Leo with regions and the Sun in Aries). What the texts read here add:
* Mār. Saṁskāra v. 7 (lines 1323 to 1384, Hindi 1118 to 1135): in garbhādhāna to annaprāśana the *maudhya* (asta), the bālya and vārdhakya, Simha-Guru, Dakṣiṇāyana and the adhika/kṣaya months are **not** kept ("*kālasya rodhāt*"); from cūḍā on they are. The Finder cannot say "for this purpose not": it has no saṁskāra presets, but if they are added the flag must be per purpose. Mār. Miśra v. 18 (5975 to 6012) lists rites without *maudhya*: Īśāna-bali, Ahi-bali, Hari's sleep and turning, *nitya-yātrā*, entering an old house, the swing festival, the *damana* planting, offering the *pavitra*, the Cāturmāsya vow, Aṣṭakā, festivals; and v. 19 the pilgrimage rule.
* **Bālya and vārdhakya: three sets of days in the texts.** Mār. (v. 7, Hindi): 7 days each, the middle of the many figures the commentary knows. K Ch. XXXIII (10881 to 10909): a fortnight before Jupiter sets, 10 days before Venus sets, 10 days after Venus rises, 8 days after Jupiter rises; K Ch. XIV (5668 to 5680) for marriage: a week after Venus rises, 8 days after Jupiter rises, 15 days before the setting. The MC (as recorded in `docs/mahadosha-vs-muhurta-chintamani.md`): Venus 3 days (east) or 10 (west) after rising, 15 (east) or 5 (west) before setting; Jupiter 15 and 15. The Finder does not do it; if it does, the length must be a choice.
* K Ch. XXXIV 11700 to 11735: *Moudyam* is harmless if one of the two is well placed (own house, exaltation, friend's sign); Venus combust is harmless when Jupiter is in the Lagna with malefics in the 3rd, 6th, 11th; Venus in the Lagna answers Jupiter combust; the *asta* "affects only Brahmins, Kṣatriyas and Vaiśyas" unless both are combust; *Samadṛṣṭi* north of the Narmadā only. None of this is in the app.
* K has no rule for Simha-Guru; Mār. v. 53 agrees with the MC on the rivers (Garga and Vasiṣṭha are quoted) and adds that in a crisis a work may be done with Jupiter in Leo after the Hari-lava (the Leo navāṁśa; the OCR of that verse is garbled).

**D2. Pariharas: K has a much larger and different set than the MC.** The levels `named`, `broad`, `all` follow the MC, and Mār. v. 25 to 27 repeat it. K Ch. XXXIV (11278 to 11823) has about 60 further rules, for example: the Moon in the 9th or 10th aspected by benefics against the janma nakṣatra and the 3rd, 5th, 7th, 10th, 19th (11294); benefics in angles and trines with malefics in the 3rd, 6th, 11th against Ekārgala (11417); Jupiter or Venus in an angle against Viṣṭi (11495) and benefics in angles against Riktā and Viṣṭi (11484); the Moon in her Varga or a friend's sign aspected by benefics against Gaṇḍānta (11660); "Viṣṭi, Vyatīpāta, Vaidhṛti and the janma nakṣatra do not prevail after midday" (11466); "Sunday, Tuesday and Saturday do not prevail after nightfall" (11476); "No day of the week is bad, provided the lord thereof is strong" (11480); "Jupiter or Venus occupying the rising sign or aspecting it counteracts the effects of an inauspicious Thithi, day, asterism or rising sign" (11518). Mār. v. 16 adds the Vedha exceptions (the Lagna lord in the 11th, the Moon aspected by a benefic, a benefic in the Lagna, a benefic's hora; the Hindi 1870 to 1880).

**D3. Regions: three texts, three assignments.** The app uses the MC's v. 69: Upagraha in Kuru and Bāhlīka, Pāta in Vaṅga, Kaliṅga and Magadha, Lattā in Saurāṣṭra and Śālva, Yāmitra in the Yamunā country. Mār. v. 16 quotes: "*lattā mālavake deśe pātaṁ kauśalake tathā / ekārgalaṁ tu kāśmīre vedhaṁ sarvatra varjayet*" (Lattā in Mālava, Pāta in Kosala, Ekārgala in Kashmir, Vedha everywhere; lines 2311 to 2312). K: Śūla, adhimāsa, Ekārgala and the blind nakṣatras north of the Sone only (11426); Paṅgu, Andha, Kāṇa and Śūnya-māsa in Magadha and Gauḍa only (11584); Samadṛṣṭi north of the Narmadā (11730); Upākarma by the Narmadā (4526); "Vara and Nakshathra Yogas are considered important in Bengal; in the Yamala country Thithi and Vara Yogas; in Kalinga Amsa Yoga; in Avanthi Vishkamba and other Yogas" (11736). `REGION_IDS` has none of Mālava, Kosala, Kashmir, the Sone, the Narmadā. A place in Europe, with no region given, counts every fault in full.

**D4. Eclipse.** Mār. v. 18: the days to leave out after an eclipse depend on its extent (3, 4, 6, 8), half that before it, and after an *utpāta* 7 days; the app (`eclipse`) keeps 7 days around a solar and 3 around a lunar eclipse (the user's text), and a 6-month nakṣatra rule (Mār. v. 17 also has six months). K's Graha-śūla (10438): 7 days before and 7 after the Moon's eclipse day.

**D5. Ardhayāma and Kāla.** The app's Ardhayāma (Sunday 4, Monday 7, Tuesday 2, Wednesday 5, Thursday 8, Friday 3, Saturday 6) is Mārtaṇḍa's; **K has a different table** (Sunday 4, Monday 3, Tuesday 2, Wednesday 1, Thursday 7, Friday 6, Saturday 5), and K's Yamagaṇḍa is the app's. K also gives the **Kālan** table (the app says "the tables are not in the text"): Sunday 1, Monday 7, Tuesday 6, Wednesday 5, Thursday 4, Friday 3, Saturday 2 of the eight periods (10554 to 10557), with a night table (10595 to 10609). K says Gulika is **not** a fault (10569, 11406); Mār. lists Kulika as one.

**D6. Tārābala.** Mār. (Gocara v. 1, 6525): only the remainders 3, 5, 7 are bad ("*tryadrīṣu śeṣe na sat*"); the Janma tārā (1) is not forbidden. K (10103 to 10160): the first cycle has all the faults; the second cycle only a half (and of the 3rd, 5th, 7th only one quarter each); **the third cycle has none**. The app's `taraOf` uses `% 9` for all 27, its usual set `[2,4,6,8,9]` leaves out the Janma tārā too, and the faults apply equally to the three cycles.

**D7. Weights.** K (11753): "Lagna, asterism, day and Thithi rank in importance in the order in which they are mentioned": nakṣatra above vāra above tithi. `FINDER_WEIGHTS` has vāra 8 above nakṣatra 4. The strength of a remedy: K says Siddha 100, Amṛta 300, Sutha 1000, Venus in the Lagna 1000, Mercury 100, Jupiter many thousands; Mār. v. 26 and the MC say Mercury 100, Venus 200, Jupiter 100 000 (in an angle or a trine). The texts do not agree.

**D8. Durmuhūrta.** The app takes the *tithyaṁśa* (a thirtieth of a tithi). Mār. v. 21 says "*muhūrtā divā duṣṭāḥ*", and its commentary: "the Sun's 14th muhūrta is the durmuhūrta, and kulika also falls in the 14th muhūrta" (my reading: a muhūrta of the day). The Sun's number 14 is the same in both, so I cannot say from the OCR that the unit differs; the other days' numbers were not readable. The MC (`mahadosha-vs-muhurta-chintamani.md`) has the day in 15 muhūrtas named by gods. K has no durmuhūrta.

**D9. Pañcaka.** The app says Bāṇa/Pañcaka "depends on the purpose" and does not work it out. Both texts give the arithmetic: K Ch. XXIX 9339 to 9357 (tithi + vāra + nakṣatra + Lagna, plus 15, 12, 10, 8 or 4, divided by 9, remainder 5 is the fault: illness, fire, royalty, thieves, death) and Mār. v. 29 (remainders 8, 2, 4, 6, 1 = Roga, Agni, Rāja, Cora, Mṛtyu; the same as K by my arithmetic: 5 − 15, 5 − 12, 5 − 10, 5 − 8, 5 − 4 modulo 9). The terms of the sum differ (Mār.'s is garbled), and Mār. makes the fault void when the Lagna is strong.

**D10. Saṅkrānti.** The app: 16′ of the Sun's longitude each side (≈ 16 ghaṭīs) and 3° at Cancer and Capricorn (≈ 3 days). Mār.: 16 + 16 ghaṭīs for the Sun, which agrees. K (10521): 30 ghaṭīs each side, **60 for the Libra entry**, and 3 days each side at Aries, Cancer, Capricorn (the app has only Cancer and Capricorn).

**D11. Shop opening and trade.** Neither text gives a verse for opening a shop. Mār. Miśra v. 3 (5400 to 5432, Hindi 5026 to 5046; the ToC has *paṇyānāṁ vikraye muhūrtaḥ*) is for buying and selling goods: the nine nakṣatras Anurādhā, Hasta, Svātī, Mūla, Aśvinī, Śravaṇa, Mṛgaśīrṣa, Puṣya, Uttara Bhādrapada; an upacaya Lagna other than Kumbha with the 2nd pure of malefics; and a verse (the ledger: Śrīpati) with the 10th or 11th Lagna, benefics in the 2nd, angles and trines, not Kumbha. The Finder's `business-opening` follows Wilhelm and MC 17 (Venus *or* Moon in the Lagna). K Ch. XXI lists 17 nakṣatras for buying and selling anything, which is not the MC's list for selling, and K says Gulika is good for trade.

**D12. Journey.** K's fruitful Lagnas include Cancer (8775); the preset takes only Taurus, Leo, Libra, Sagittarius and keeps Cancer as middling.

**D13. Bhāva list for the wedding.** The preset has the Moon and malefics not in the Lagna, Saturn not in the 12th, Mars not in the 8th or 10th, Venus not in the 6th, the Moon not in the 6th or 8th, and no benefic in the 8th. My reading of Mār. v. 24 (doubtful, see the table) also names the Moon in the 12th, Mercury in the 10th, Venus in the 3rd, Rāhu in the 4th, all grahas in the 7th. Only the 6th, 7th and the Lagna are certain; the rest needs the printed page.

**D14. Vedha of nakṣatras (marriage).** Mār. v. 14 to 15 gives the Saptaśalākā diagram and the pāda-vedha rule in words (the MC's cakras are pictures); it applies to marriage only. The Hindi edition at 1830 to 1860 prints the diagram.

**D15. Saṁskāras.** No preset exists for upanayana, cūḍā, annaprāśana, karṇavedha, garbhādhāna and the rest, though both texts give them.

**D16. The Moon's good houses.** The app's default `[1,3,6,7,10,11]` leaves out the bright-half 2, 5, 9 that Mār. adds ("*śukle naveṣudvigaḥ*"). The user can tick them.

**D17. Gaṇḍānta of the tithi.** Mār. v. 19 (my reading, by the pattern nakṣatra 4 = 2 + 2, tithi 2 = 1 + 1, Lagna 1 = ½ + ½) and the MC (as recorded in the new doc) give 1 ghaṭī each side for the tithi; the app takes 2 each side, as Wilhelm does.
