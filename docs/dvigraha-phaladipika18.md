# Two grahas together, and the Moon seen by the others (Phaladīpikā 18)

Source: the Phaladīpikā of Mantreśvara with the Hindi commentary of the edition (`phaldipika_202310`, a scan of the print with a text layer), chapter 18 "Dvigrahayoga-phala", 17 verses. The code is `src/lib/dvigrahaPhala.ts`,
the view ANALYSIS → Conjunctions (v3.81). The Sanskrit is shown as the text layer has it; a few letters are misread (it is said in the view).

## The text

| Verses | What |
|---|---|
| 18.1–5 | Two grahas in one sign: the Sun with each of the other six, the Moon with the other five, Mars with four, Mercury with three, Jupiter with Venus and Saturn, Venus with Saturn: 21 pairs. With more than two grahas the results of the pairs are to be combined (18.5) |
| 18.6–11 | The Moon in each of the twelve signs seen by Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn and the Sun, in that order: 72 results |
| 18.12–15 | The Moon in the navāṁśa of Mars, Venus, Mercury, the Moon, the Sun, Jupiter and Saturn seen by the Sun, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus and Saturn: 42 results |
| 18.16 | What was said of the Moon by the sign is the dvādaśāṁśa result, and what was said by the navāṁśa is the navāṁśa result |
| 18.17 | If the Moon is vargottama, in its own navāṁśa or in another's, the good results are the greatest, the middle, the least (the bad the other way round); a strong lord of the navāṁśa holds back the results by the sign and gives only those of the navāṁśa |

## What the app does

* Finds the pairs of the seven grahas in one sign and gives the result of each, with the verse; a sign with more than two is named, because the pairs are to be combined.
* Gives the result for the Moon's sign for each graha that aspects it (whole signs, Rāhu and Ketu left out), and for the Moon's navāṁśa and its lord for each graha that aspects it.
* Names whether the Moon is vargottama, in its own navāṁśa or another's, with the weighing of 18.17.

## Not done

* **18.5**: with more than two grahas in a sign the text says to combine the results of the pairs; the app names the crowded sign and lists the pairs but does not combine them.
* **The end of 18.15**: the text says that the same is to be imagined for the Sun and the other grahas in the navāṁśas of the grahas, as for the Moon. It gives no table for them, so the app does the Moon only.
* **Rāhu and Ketu** are not in the chapter and are not in the view.
* **Other classics** (the Sārāvalī, the Jātakapārijāta) have their own tables for grahas together; they are not built.

* The strength of the lord of the navāṁśa (18.17) is not judged: the text gives no measure.
* The summaries are short; the Hindi commentary has the full wording. In the table of the Moon in Taurus the commentary's line for Saturn is garbled in the scan and is taken from the Sanskrit verse (wealthy).
* "Seen by" is the aspect of the graha (the 7th for all, Mars the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, Saturn the 3rd and 10th); the text says only *dṛṣṭa*.
