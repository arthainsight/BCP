# Wishes about the Rāśi chart (D1) in the Muhūrta Finder

The book (Ernst Wilhelm, *Classical Muhurta*, chapter 15 "Lagna" and chapter 16 "Rasi and Navamsa") names places of the Lagna and
the grahas in the Rāśi chart that are favourable. It gives **no weights** for them: the only numbers it gives about importance are
that the Sun is 3/5 as significant as the Moon, that the Vara/Nakshatra yogas are three times as strong as the Vara/Tithi yogas
and the Vara/Tithi/Nakshatra yogas three times as strong again, and an order of priority (the Pañcāṅga, the
Vara/Tithi/Nakshatra yoga, Tara, then the lords and the Mahādoṣas, then the chart). The scale of the Pañcāṅga wishes
(tithi 1 … Chandrabala 100) is the user's, from the Atharvaṇa Jyotiṣa as the Phalita Navaratna Saṅgraha extends it; it has nothing
for the chart. So each wish about the chart is worth **one point**, added to the score. They limit nothing; they are off at the start.

| Wish | What it asks | In the book |
|---|---|---|
| Lagna of a benefic | The Lagna in Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Virgo, Libra, Sagittarius or Pisces; Cancer not when the Moon is a malefic | "The Rasis of the benefic planets … are generally favorable"; the Moon a benefic 120°–240° from the Sun, a malefic within 60° of it, "when the Moon is a malefic, Cancer Lagna is best avoided" |
| Navāṁśa Lagna of a benefic | The Navāṁśa Lagna in the same signs | "The Navamsa Lagna should be that of a benefic planet"; "even more important than the Lagna itself" |
| More benefics than malefics in the angles | In the 1st, 4th, 7th, 10th together more benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, the Moon when a benefic) than malefics (Sun, Mars, Saturn, Rāhu, Ketu, the Moon when a malefic) | "A good rule of thumb … there should always be more benefics in the angles than malefics" |
| The Moon in an upachaya | The Moon in the 3rd, 10th or 11th from the Lagna | "The Moon is very favorable in Upachayas (outside of the 6th) … any Lagna is rendered favorable if the Moon is in an Upachaya, especially the 11th" |
| 8th and 12th empty | No graha in the 8th or 12th | "For beneficial events, the 8th and 12th Bhavas are best left unoccupied" |

Left out (the exceptions the book gives, and the rest of chapter 16): Jupiter or Venus in or aspecting the Lagna overcoming a Lagna
of a malefic; Jupiter and Venus best in the Lagna and angles and worst in the 6th; the Moon worst in the 8th, then the 12th; the
judgment of a planet as well or poorly disposed (dignities, aspects, combustion, the 6th/8th/12th in the Navāṁśa from the Rāśi, the
last degree); the Fatal Degrees (Mṛtyubhāga) of the planets and the Lagna; the Śūnya Rāśis of the tithis and of the months as Lagna;
the blind, deaf and lame Rāśis; the Ashtakavarga of the Lagna, Sun and Moon. The Fatal Degrees and the Śūnya Lagnas have tables in
the book and could be added.

The wishes are tested by hand (the Lagna and the Moon at made-up places) and against the chart of the app at the middle of each
stretch the search finds.
