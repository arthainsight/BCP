# The results of the upagrahas in the houses (BPHS 25), after the Sanskrit

Source: Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra chapter 25 in Devanagari with the verse numbers (the text of enjoylearningsanskrit.com), 87 verses. The code is `src/lib/upagrahaResults.ts`, the verses `src/lib/upagrahaVerses.ts`,
the view ANALYSIS → Upagrahas (v3.80).

**Note on the chapter.** Chapter 25 is not the seven grahas in the houses: it opens by saying the house results of the seven grahas, the Sun and the rest, have been told, and it gives those of the non-luminous bodies.
The seven grahas in the houses are in the chapters of the houses, 12–23 (not built).

## The text

* **25.2–85**: seven bodies × twelve houses = 84 verses, in blocks of twelve in the order of the houses: Dhūma 2–13, Pāta 14–25, Paridhi 26–37, Cāpa 38–49, Ketu 50–61, Gulika 62–73, Prāṇapada 74–85.
* **25.86–87**: the close. The results are told by the position of the body, by the exact aspects on it, and by discerning its strength and weakness.

## What the app does

* The points are those the app already has: Dhūma (Dhuma), Pāta (Vyatipāta), Paridhi (Parivesha), Cāpa (Indrachāpa), Ketu (Upaketu), Gulika (at the moment of Saturn's part chosen in the settings) and Prāṇapada (the special lagna). Māndi is in the app and not in the text.
* For each body the sign, the house from the Lagna and the verse for that house: the Sanskrit as the text has it, the IAST and a one-line summary.
* A test checks the 84 verses: each body in each house once, the verses in order, and that the verses of a block name that body.

## Not done

* How strong the body is and what aspects it gets (25.87): the text gives no measure.
* The luminous grahas in the houses (BPHS 12–23).
* The summaries are short and mine; the verse decides. Verse 29 (Paridhi in the 4th) is very brief in the print and its summary says so.
