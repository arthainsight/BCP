# The classics of Vedic astrology: the list, and where the app has read them

**The rule of the project:** the original text decides. Where a text says something other than PyJHora, JHora, a modern book or the app itself,
the text is the default of the app and the other way is a choice in the Settings (so the Chara daśā, v3.67: the Jaimini Sūtras are the start,
"my own choices" keep the old conventions; the Sthira daśā: the PyJHora way is the second choice).

Dates and authors are the traditional ones; most are disputed. "Found" is an edition in Sanskrit or Hindi on archive.org whose OCR is text
(Devanagari); the identifier is for `https://archive.org/details/<identifier>` and `…/download/<identifier>/<file>_djvu.txt`. Nothing is
"read" until a row of the last column says so.

**Read** means that a rule of the app was set beside the verse and compared. Of this corpus nothing is read yet: it is on the disk of the
session, not in the repository. The first uses are the yoga definitions (Sārāvalī, Bṛhat Jātaka, Sarvārtha Cintāmaṇi, Uttara Kālāmṛta against
`yogasClassical.ts`), the dignities (Bṛhat Jātaka, Sārāvalī), the Tājika yogas and the Muntha of the annual chart (Tājika Nīlakaṇṭhī against
`annualDasha.ts`), and the Praśna rules (Praśna Mārga).

The daśās that the classics have and the app has not are in `dasha-candidates.md`.
