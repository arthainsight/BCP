# The Mahādoṣas of the Muhūrta Finder

The Muhūrta Finder can keep the stretches it finds clear of the great faults (Mahādoṣas) of a muhūrta. The list of 21 is the
one the user supplied ("These 21 Mahādoṣas should be avoided for all important occasions"); the definitions and tables are
those of **Ernst Wilhelm, *Classical Muhurta*** (Kala Occult Publishers), chapter 17 "Doshas" (Rishi Nārada's twenty-one great
detriments) and the chapters it points to (Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, Tithyamsa, the marriage muhūrta), and of his article
**"Muhurta Yogas"** (Combinations of Vara, Tithi & Nakshatra). Where the user's text and the book differ, the user's text is
followed and the difference is noted below. **Nothing is filled in from memory:** a doṣa whose table or definition is in neither
is listed in the page as "Not worked out" with its reason.

## Where the user's text and the book differ

* #14: the user's list is Kulika, Yamaghaṇṭa, Kāla Velā, Ardhayāma and Rāhu Kāla; the book's Varadosha is the unfavourable Vara/Tithi/Nakṣatra yogas (its Ardhayāma is in the marriage chapter). The user's list is followed, and the weekday–nakṣatra yogas of the article (Yamaghaṇṭa, Mṛtyu) are in the Pañcāṅga Doṣa.
* #15: the book's item is Ekārgalā alone; the other Daśa Doṣas are in its marriage chapter.
* #17 and #18 are described differently.
* #3: the user's text says "the Sun at 0° in any sign" with no length; the book gives 16 nāḍīs and 3°.

## Notes

* With all of them chosen at once less than a twentieth of the time is left; the choice is the user's.
* A doṣa of the Ascendant (2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 15 Jāmitra, 19) makes the search look every 5 minutes instead of every 15.
* The Aṣṭama Lagna needs the birth chart: it is the chart that is open.
* The Vyatipāta and Vaidhṛti here are the equal declinations of the Sun and the Moon (Krānti Sāmya), not the 17th and 27th yogas of the panchāṅga, which are in the Pañcāṅga Doṣa above.
* **The classics decide.** Since v3.61 the Sanskrit text of the Muhūrtacintāmaṇi is the reference and Wilhelm's book the second: see `mahadosha-vs-muhurta-chintamani.md` for each doṣa beside its verse, the neutralisations (pariharas) and the regions the finder can apply, and what still differs.
* Added from the Muhūrtacintāmaṇi: **Dagdha tithi** (v. 66) and **Guru and Śukra** (asta of Jupiter and Venus, Jupiter in Leo or Capricorn; 1.46–51), both in the blemishes outside the list of 21. Saṅgraha no longer counts Mercury and Jupiter (v. 45).
* The Viṣanāḍī of the nakṣatras is the table of all 27 of the Muhūrtacintāmaṇi (v. 49–51), not Wilhelm's.
* Sources: Ernst Wilhelm, *Classical Muhurta*; Ernst Wilhelm, "Muhurta Yogas" (vedic-astrology.net/Articles/Muhurta-Yogas.pdf). The tables of the Viṣanāḍī of the tithis and nakṣatras are typed in `mahadosha.test.ts` as the book prints them, and the formula of the code is compared with them over the whole circle.
