# The Mahādoṣas of the Muhūrta Finder

The Muhūrta Finder can keep the stretches it finds clear of the great faults (Mahādoṣas) of a muhūrta. The list of 21 and the
definitions are those of the teaching of muhūrta that the user supplied (the text begins "These 21 Mahādoṣas should be avoided
for all important occasions"). **Nothing is filled in from memory:** a doṣa whose table is not in the text, or whose sources
disagree, is listed in the page as "Not worked out" with its reason, and waits for a source.

## Not worked out, and why

The sources searched for the figures that the text does not give (the partial ghaṭīs of the inauspicious yogas, the Dūrmuhūrta
tables, the Kulika and Kāla Velā tables) disagree with each other; the page does not choose one. Give the tables from the
original and they can be added.

## Notes

* With all fifteen chosen at once less than a twentieth of the time is left; the choice is the user's.
* A doṣa of the Ascendant (2, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 19) makes the search look every 5 minutes instead of every 15.
* The Aṣṭama Lagna needs the birth chart: it is the chart that is open.
* The Vyatipāta and Vaidhṛti here are the equal declinations of the Sun and the Moon (Krānti Sāmya), not the 17th and 27th
  yogas of the panchāṅga, which are in the Pañcāṅga Doṣa above.
