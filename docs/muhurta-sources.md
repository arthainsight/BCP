## Passages read

### Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, prakaraṇa 1 (Śubhāśubha), verse 17: opening a shop (vikraya-vipaṇi-muhūrta)
The original of Wilhelm's "Store/Business Opening". Verse (OCR of `in.ernet.dli.2015.553968`, about lines 3955–3982 of the text; Hindi in the Khemraj edition around line 1776): *pūrvādītīśa-kṛśānu-sārpa-yama-bhe kendra-trikoṇe śubhaiḥ ṣaṭtryāyeṣv-aśubhair vinā ghaṭatanuṃ sad-vikrayaḥ sat-tithau / rikta-bhauma-ghaṭān vinā ca vipaṇir mitra-dhruva-kṣipra-bhe lagne candra-site vyayāṣṭa-rahitaiḥ pāpaiḥ śubhair dvyāyakhe*.
- Selling begins in the three Pūrvās, Viśākhā, Kṛttikā, Āśleṣā, Bharaṇī, with benefics in the angles and trines and malefics in the 3rd, 6th, 11th, not Kumbha rising, in a good tithi.
- The shop opens, except in a Riktā tithi, on Tuesday or in Kumbha rising, in Mitra (Anurādhā), Dhruva and Kṣipra nakṣatras, with **the Moon and Venus in the Lagna** (*candra-site*; the Hindi: "Moon, Venus in the lagna"), no malefics in the 8th and 12th, benefics in the 2nd, 11th and 10th. Commentary: the Moon in the Lagna is forbidden in all works, but here it is good because it lords the merchants and is with Venus.
- Wilhelm has "Venus **or** the Moon"; the app (`business-opening`) follows Wilhelm, which is looser than the text.
- Pīyūṣadhārā's list of the nakṣatras of the shop (OCR): Citrā, Anurādhā, Revatī, Rohiṇī, the three Uttarās, Aśvinī, Puṣya, Hasta, and Mṛgaśīrṣa by the mild group; this is where Wilhelm's list of eleven comes from. Reading depends on garbled OCR.
- Cited from Śrīpati (Jyotiṣa Ratnamālā): Lagna the 10th or 11th with benefics in the wealth house, angles and trines, not Kumbha rising (`martanda.txt`, about line 5420).

### Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, prakaraṇa 1, verses 46–51: Jupiter and Venus afflicted; Jupiter in Leo
- 46–47: the acts forbidden in the combustion, childhood and old age of Jupiter and Venus (and in the intercalary month): new wells, gardens and tanks, house entry, initiation, marriage, tonsure, first visit to a deity or pilgrimage, renunciation, coronation, **journey**, ear-piercing, installation of a deity, upanayana, and so on. A business or shop is **not** named.
- 48: the same acts are forbidden when Jupiter is in Siṁha (Leo) or Makara (Capricorn); with variants (retrograde and fast Jupiter, Jupiter in the Sun's sign). Rājamārtaṇḍa (quoted in the Pīyūṣadhārā) lists journey, tonsure, marriage, ear-piercing, house entry, building, beginning of study, upanayana, installation, wearing gems and gold.
- 49: *siṁhe guro siṁhalave vivāho neṣṭo 'tha godottarataś ca yāvat / bhāgīrathī-yāmyataraṃ hi doṣo nānyatra deśe tapane 'pi meṣe*. Marriage is not wished with Jupiter in Leo, in the Leo navāṁśa (the 5th, 13°20′–16°40′); the fault is in the land from north of the Godāvarī to south of the Bhāgīrathī (the Ganges), not elsewhere, and not when the Sun is in Aries.
- 50: *maghādi-pañca-pādeṣu guruḥ sarvatra nindita / gaṅgā-godāntaraṃ hitvā śeṣāṅghriṣu na doṣakṛt*. Jupiter in the five pādas from Maghā (Maghā 1–4 and Pūrva Phalgunī 1, that is 0°–16°40′ of Leo, Wilhelm's "first five navāṁśas") is condemned everywhere; in the other pādas the fault is only between the Ganges and the Godāvarī.
- 51: with the Sun in Aries marriage and upanayana are good even between the Ganges and the Godāvarī; all Simha-Guru is to be avoided in Kaliṅga, Gauḍa and Gurjara.
- The Pīyūṣadhārā (OCR garbled; my reading): the exceptions by region and by the Sun in Aries free **marriage and upanayana only**; other auspicious acts remain forbidden, since an exception reaches only what it names. Vasiṣṭha is quoted: *bhāgīrathyuttare deśe gautamyā dakṣiṇe taṭe / vivāhe vratabandhe vā siṁhasthejye na duṣyati*. Rājamārtaṇḍa: *siṁharāśau tu siṁhāṁśe yadā bhavati vākpatiḥ / sarvadeśeṣv ayaṃ tyājyo dampatyor nidhanapradaḥ*.
- Śaunaka, quoted in Muhūrta Mārtaṇḍa (about line 3666 of the OCR text): *siṁhastha-deva-gurau meṣasthā yadi …* (Jupiter in Leo, Sun in Aries is allowed).

### Muhūrtacintāmaṇi, prakaraṇa 1: the nakṣatras and their works (Khemraj edition, about lines 1580–1600)
- Maghā: agriculture, **trade (vyāpāra)**, cows, grain, war-works, marriage, dance, song. Mūla: agriculture, **commerce (vāṇijya)**, harsh works, medicine, dance, crafts, treaties, writing. Svātī: installation, upanayana, **trade**, parting the hair, ornaments, quarrels, elephant-works, farming, tonsure. Viśākhā: clothes, ornament-trade, storing juices and grain, dance, song, crafts, writing. Jyeṣṭhā: cruel works, weapons, **trade**, cows and buffaloes, water-works, music, iron and stone works. Āśleṣā: lies, vice, gambling, metallurgy, medicine, war, dispute, alchemy, **trade**.
- Ārdrā: flags, arches, war, walls, weapons, treaties, dissension, poison and juices. Trade is not among them.
- The rule on buying and selling (verse 16): the nakṣatras for buying are not those for selling; it is a rule for the beginning of a trade, not for everyday dealing. Buying: Revatī, Śatabhiṣā, Aśvinī, Svātī, Śravaṇa (and Citrā by Dīpikā); selling: the three Pūrvās, Viśākhā, Kṛttikā, Āśleṣā, Bharaṇī.
